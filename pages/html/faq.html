Help

Frequently asked questions

Ordering & delivery

Where do you deliver?+

We deliver across Vadodara city from our cloud kitchen in Akota — including Alkapuri, Old Padra Road, Race Course, Gotri, Vasna-Bhayli, Manjalpur, Karelibaug, Fatehgunj, Sama, Nizampura and Waghodia Road. Enter your address at checkout to confirm delivery for your street.

What are your opening hours?+

We take orders from 11 am to 9 pm, every day of the week. Subscription lunches arrive between 12 and 2 pm and dinners between 7 and 9 pm.

Where can I get healthy food in Vadodara?+

Leafli is a Vadodara kitchen dedicated to salads and healthy bowls — high-protein vegetarian salads, chicken salads and fruit bowls with calories and allergens listed for every dish. Order online at leafli.in for delivery anywhere in Vadodara.

Can I order salads for my office in Vadodara?+

Yes. We deliver team lunches and bulk salad orders to offices across Vadodara. Contact us a day ahead for large orders so we can schedule them.

How long does delivery take?+

Most orders are assembled within 15–20 minutes and then handed to a rider. You can track your rider live from your order page.

Can I order on Zomato?+

Yes, Leafli is on Zomato too. Ordering directly on leafli.in gets you subscriptions and saved addresses.

How do I pay?+

Pay online with UPI, cards, netbanking or wallets through Razorpay, or choose pay on delivery.

Food & freshness

Why are dressings packed separately?+

So nothing goes soggy on the way. Toss the dressing in just before you eat for the best crunch.

How fresh is the food?+

Bowls are assembled to order. Sauces and roasted components are made in small dated batches, kept at 4°C or below, and never used past their use-by.

Do you list allergens?+

Yes — every dish page lists its allergens. Our kitchen also handles nuts, peanuts, sesame, soy, gluten, egg, dairy and mustard, so tell us about allergies in your order notes.

Do you have vegetarian options?+

Most of our menu is vegetarian, clearly marked with the green veg symbol. Egg and non-veg dishes are marked too.

Subscriptions

How do subscriptions work?+

Buy a pack of bowls, pick your delivery days and slot, and we deliver one bowl per delivery day. Choose your bowl for any day or let the chef rotate the menu.

Can I pause or skip?+

Yes — skip a day, pause your plan or change your bowl until 9 pm the day before. Pausing extends your plan's validity.

What happens to unused bowls?+

Bowls must be used within the plan's validity period. See our refund policy for cancellations.
