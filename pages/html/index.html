The Leafli promise

We run our kitchen on written recipes, timers and thermometers — so your lunch is fresh, safe, and exactly as good as last time.

0soggy leaves

Dressings travel separately

Caesar, pesto, mango-chilli and Ghibriche go in their own sealed cups. Croutons and granola go on last, so they still crunch.

4°C or colder

Cold from prep to door

Sauces and cut produce are kept chilled and labelled with their prep time and use-by. Nothing goes out past its date.

165grams, exactly

Weighed, not eyeballed

Every component is weighed to the gram, so the bowl you love on Monday tastes the same on Friday.

13small-batch preps

Sauces made in-house

Dressings, pesto and slow-roasted potatoes are made in small dated batches, following our own recipes.

From our board to your desk in three easy steps.

    Pick your bowl
